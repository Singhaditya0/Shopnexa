Who we are

ShopNexa was founded in 2026 with a simple observation: online shopping in India has become overwhelming. The same product appears at five different prices across five platforms, "limited time" banners never expire, and inflated MRPs make a 70% discount look extraordinary when it is actually ordinary.

We built ShopNexa as a neutral layer between shoppers and marketplaces. We are not a store, we do not hold inventory, and we do not process payments. We are a research desk — our only job is to tell you whether something is worth buying today, or worth waiting for.

Our mission

Find the best before you buy.
To give every shopper access to the kind of price intelligence that was previously available only to professional buyers — clearly presented, completely free, and always honest about how we earn.
  • Make genuine price transparency the default, not a premium feature.
  • Protect shoppers from inflated MRPs, fake urgency and misleading discount claims.
  • Recommend fewer products, but only the ones we would confidently buy ourselves.
  • Keep the platform free for shoppers, forever.

How ShopNexa works

01
We aggregate
Our systems continuously collect product listings, prices and offers from major Indian e-commerce marketplaces and trusted sellers.
02
We verify
Listings are cross-checked against price history, seller ratings, return policies and product specifications to filter out misleading offers.
03
We curate
Only deals that genuinely offer value make it to ShopNexa. Everything else is rejected — quantity never beats quality.
04
You decide
You compare, click through to the official store, and complete your purchase directly with the retailer. We never handle your payment.

How we make money

ShopNexa is free to use. When you click a product link and complete a purchase on a partner store, we may earn a small affiliate commission from that retailer. This commission is paid by the seller — it never increases the price you pay.

Crucially, commissions do not influence our rankings. A product with zero commission can and does outrank a high-commission alternative if it is the better buy. You can read the complete breakdown on our Affiliate Disclosure page.
