AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE

How we earn — stated openly.

ShopNexa is a free platform funded by affiliate commissions. This page explains exactly how those partnerships work and, just as importantly, how they do not affect what we recommend.

🕐 Last updated: 15 February 2026
Disclosure statement
ShopNexa participates in various affiliate marketing programmes. This means we may earn a commission when you click a link on our site and complete a qualifying purchase with a partner retailer. You never pay more because of this. The commission is paid by the retailer from their own margin.

1What affiliate marketing means

Affiliate marketing is a standard, widely used model in which a publisher — in this case ShopNexa — refers a customer to a retailer and receives a referral fee if a sale takes place. It is the reason we can keep price research, deal curation and comparison tools free for every shopper.

2Does it cost you anything?

No. The price you pay is identical whether you reach the retailer through ShopNexa or by typing their address directly into your browser. Affiliate commissions come out of the retailer's marketing budget, not your wallet. In many cases our links also carry exclusive coupons that make the price lower than the public rate.

3Editorial independence

Our rankings, ratings and "best pick" labels are decided before commercial terms are considered. A product with no affiliate programme at all can — and regularly does — outrank a high-commission alternative.

  • Commission rates never determine placement, ordering or star ratings.
  • We publish negative or cautionary notes on partner products where they are warranted.
  • Sponsored or paid placements, when they occur, are clearly labelled as such.
  • We remove partners who engage in fake MRP inflation or misleading discount practices.

4Programmes we may participate in

ShopNexa may be enrolled in affiliate and referral programmes operated by major Indian and international e-commerce marketplaces, brand storefronts, electronics and fashion retailers, and third-party affiliate networks that aggregate multiple merchants.

Partner relationships change over time as programmes open and close. Regardless of the network involved, the disclosure on this page applies to every monetised link across the platform.

5Tracking and cookies

When you click an outbound product link, the destination retailer may place a cookie on your device to attribute a resulting purchase to ShopNexa. This cookie typically stores a referral identifier and a timestamp. It does not give us access to your payment details, your order contents or your personal account with that retailer. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

6Accuracy of prices and offers

Deals move fast. A price or coupon shown on ShopNexa may expire or change before you reach checkout. Always verify the final amount on the retailer's own page before completing payment. We correct reported errors as quickly as possible but cannot be held liable for third-party pricing changes.

7No liability for third-party purchases

ShopNexa is not the seller of record. Delivery timelines, product quality, warranty, replacements, refunds and customer service remain the sole responsibility of the retailer you purchase from. Any dispute regarding an order must be raised with that retailer directly.

8Your support matters

If you find our research useful, using our links is the simplest way to support the platform — it costs you nothing and helps us continue tracking prices, verifying sellers and publishing honest recommendations.
